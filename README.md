# Designed to Exist

**Be seen. Be remembered. Exist.**

Designed to Exist (DTE) is a small design studio that makes animated portfolio websites and GitHub profiles for people who want to be seen online, and remembered. Nothing is built from a template: every site is made around the person behind it, and the finished files are handed over to the client to keep.

- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/designed_toexist
- GitHub: https://github.com/designedtoexist

---

## What we make

**Portfolio websites**
Animated, scroll-driven sites that tell your story and show your projects, built around you.

**GitHub profiles**
Animated READMEs, custom SVG heroes, and a look that makes recruiters stop scrolling.

**Yours, for keeps**
You receive the real files and go live on your own domain. No lock-in, and no monthly fees to us.

## How it works

1. **Brief.** Message us on Instagram and tell us who you are.
2. **Concept.** One bold direction, made only for you.
3. **Build.** Animated, responsive, and polished down to the pixel.
4. **Launch.** You receive the files and go live on your own domain.

## About this website

This repository is the DTE studio site itself: a single, scroll-animated page written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no build step and no dependencies.

The page moves through these sections:

| Section | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hero** | "Designed to Exist" headline with the logo disc, orbiting rings and a warm glow |
| **Not another template.** | A paragraph that reveals word by word on scroll, next to a website sketch that draws itself line by line. A cursor then clicks its button, which lights up amber |
| **Services** | The two things we make, plus what you get at handoff |
| **Sites we've made** | A pinned showcase: project names on the left and a circular "lens" on the right that wipes between live previews of each site |
| **Made just for you** | A short statement section |
| **How it works** | The four-step process: Brief, Concept, Build, Launch |
| **Ready to exist?** | The closing call to action, pointing to Instagram and GitHub |

### Look and feel

A warm mocha and amber palette taken from the logo, with Cormorant Garamond serif headings. The logo is a black cat resting on the letters DTE, with a glowing cursor and a gold sparkle. The sparkle and cursor ideas are reused across the site as small details.
